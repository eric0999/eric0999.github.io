TANK COMMAND: LIVE MAP

Your city is the battlefield.

Tank Command: Live Map turns real-world streets into a live tank-warfare arena. Built on real OpenStreetMap road data, every match plays out on actual roads and intersections instead of a fixed arena.

Real-world map roads Single-player campaign LAN multiplayer Online PvP & co-op
Core Gameplay

Command your tank, wave after wave

🎯

Campaign Waves

Fight through escalating waves of NPC tanks, including special waves and mini-bosses, as you push toward the next checkpoint.

🛡️

Checkpoints & Respawn

Fall in battle and redeploy from your last checkpoint via a cargo-plane drop-in, with a brief respawn cooldown and invulnerability window to get back in the fight.

🪙

Coins & Medic Drops

Destroy enemy tanks for coin and medic drops, then spend your earnings on new tanks and upgrades in the Military Workshop.

🔧

Tanks & Weapons

Unlock tank types across tiers, each with its own stats, weapon loadout, and color livery, and level them up as you progress.

Powered by real map data

Tank movement, pathing, and NPC AI all run on a road graph built directly from OpenStreetMap data — tanks patrol, hunt, and maneuver along real streets and intersections, not a hand-built game map.

Play Your Way

Solo, LAN, or online

Single Player

Take on the full wave-based campaign solo, unlocking tanks and upgrades as you climb through checkpoints.

LAN Local Multiplayer

One phone hosts the room over the local network — no internet required. Play Campaign co-op with friends, or go head-to-head in Deathmatch PvP.

Online Multiplayer

Connect over the internet for Firebase-backed online PvP and co-op battles against other players.
